================================================================================
SLATEPAD - The Fast, No-Nonsense Sticky Note App
================================================================================
What is this?
It's a simple, super-fast note app for Windows.
No tabs. No AI Copilot buttons. No cloud login. No complicated menus.
Just open it, jot down your thoughts, and keep working.

================================================================================
WHY DID WE MAKE THIS? (The Problem with Windows Notepad)
================================================================================
If you've used the new Windows 11 Notepad recently, you probably noticed:
  - It opens slow.
  - It forces you to use tabs when you just want a quick scratchpad.
  - It puts AI / Copilot buttons everywhere.
  - If you want to keep a note on top of your screen while looking at a website,
    you can't! Notepad gets buried behind your browser every time you click away.

SlatePad fixes all of that. It does one job, and it does it perfectly.

================================================================================
THE 4 MAIN FEATURES (Explained Simply)
================================================================================

1. ALWAYS ON TOP (Never gets buried!)
   - When you open SlatePad, it automatically floats on top of all other windows.
   - Great for taking notes while watching a video, coding, writing emails,
     or reading an article.
   - Want it to go back to being a normal window?
     Just press: Ctrl + T
   - Press Ctrl + T again anytime to pin it right back to the top.
   - The title bar tells you clearly: [Pin: ON] or [Pin: OFF].

2. SMART AUTO-SAVE (You will never lose your notes)
   - You don't have to remember to press "Save".
   - The app waits until you take a 5-second break from typing, then saves
     everything automatically to a simple file called "notes.txt".
   - How it works:
       * You type a note.
       * You pause for 5 seconds -> It saves!
       * If you type, wait 4 seconds, type one more letter, and stop,
         it smartly resets the countdown and saves 5 seconds after that last letter.
   - The title bar shows you what's happening:
       * "SlatePad * [Pin: ON] (Saving in 5s...)" while you're writing.
       * "SlatePad [Pin: ON] - Saved (17:30:00)" once it's safely saved.
   - Even if you abruptly close the window or shut down, it automatically
     saves any unsaved text right before closing.

3. INSTANT STARTUP & ZERO LAG
   - Opens in less than a blink of an eye (under 5 milliseconds).
   - Uses virtually zero computer memory (RAM).
   - It's a single, tiny program file you can put anywhere on your computer.

4. EASY ON THE EYES (Dark Mode)
   - Built with a sleek dark theme so you don't get blinded at night.
   - Clean, readable text with comfortable spacing from the edges.

================================================================================
HANDY KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS
================================================================================
  Ctrl + T  -->  Toggle "Always On Top" (Pin / Unpin)
  Ctrl + S  -->  Save immediately right now (if you don't want to wait 5 seconds)
  Ctrl + A  -->  Select all text
